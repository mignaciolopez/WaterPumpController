  safety stops (high level, max on-time, faults) still act immediately
- `IClock` gained `LocalHour()` (for night rule)
- `.gitattributes` added (LF in repo)
- ESP32 env now builds with `-std=gnu++17` (was the Arduino default gnu++11)
